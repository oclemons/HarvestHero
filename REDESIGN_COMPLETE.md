## 🎨 GLASS EFFECTS SYSTEM

### **Level 1 - Soft Glass**
- Light translucency
- Subtle border
- Thin highlight edge
- Minimal depth
- **Used on:** Secondary surfaces (cards, tables, forms)

### **Level 2 - Reflective Glass**
- Stronger depth perception
- Visible highlight band
- Layered borders
- Environment reflection
- **Used on:** Important surfaces (navigation, modals, settings)

### **Level 3 - Hero Mirror**
- Strongest reflective appearance
- Polished glass with environmental reflection
- Lighting gradients
- Glass depth with layering
- **Used on:** Signature components (login, dashboard, AI)
