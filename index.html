macOS: first-launch warning — how to open

Because this build isn't code-signed yet, macOS shows a warning the first time you open Harvest Hero:

"HarvestHero cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software."

You only have to do this once per Mac:

  1. Download and unzip the release.
  2. Drag HarvestHero.app to /Applications.
  3. In Finder, right-click HarvestHero.app and choose Open.
  4. Confirm Open in the dialog that appears.

macOS remembers your choice. Future launches are just a normal double-click. If you missed the right-click step, you can also approve the app from System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Windows: first-launch warning — how to open

Because this build isn't code-signed yet, Windows SmartScreen shows a blue popup the first time you run HarvestHero.exe:

"Windows protected your PC."

You only have to do this once per PC:

  1. Click More info on the SmartScreen popup.
  2. Click Run anyway.

Windows remembers your choice. Future launches are a normal double-click.
