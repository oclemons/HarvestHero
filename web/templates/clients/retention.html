Client retention review

Review every semester. Identifying data is due for removal six calendar months after the last completed visit. For clients who never visited, the clock starts when Admin deactivates the record.

Removing a due record deletes its student ID, birth date, dietary notes and individual visit history. Monthly organization-level visit and pounds totals remain. This cannot be undone from the live database. An Admin must confirm each removal; older encrypted backups may still contain the original data until their approved retention period ends.
